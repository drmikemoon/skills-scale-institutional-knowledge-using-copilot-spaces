# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation hub. This repository captures the project management approach OctoAcme uses to launch, deliver, and improve work across the project lifecycle. The goal is to make the process clear, repeatable, and easy to follow for project managers, product leads, engineers, QA partners, and stakeholders.

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, phase-based project lifecycle that keeps work aligned with customer value and delivery goals:

1. Initiation: confirm the need, define success, and align stakeholders before committing resources.
2. Planning: shape the work into milestones, dependencies, priorities, and delivery commitments.
3. Execution: track progress, manage risks, resolve blockers, and keep quality high through delivery.
4. Release: prepare the deployment, communicate readiness, and support a safe release.
5. Close and Continuous Improvement: capture outcomes, review lessons, and turn feedback into improvements.

This process creates clear ownership, consistent communication, and measurable progress at every stage of the lifecycle.

## OctoAcme Project Management Principles

- Customer-first: prioritize customer value, usability, and business outcomes.
- Iterative delivery: deliver small, testable increments with regular feedback.
- Clear ownership: define who is responsible for decisions, execution, and follow-up.
- Data-informed decisions: use evidence, traction, and feedback to guide adjustments.
- Psychological safety: encourage open communication, learning, and continuous improvement.

## Core Roles

- Project Manager (PM): coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communication.
- Product Manager (PdM): defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success.
- Developers: implement features and maintain quality through execution.
- QA/Testing: validate work against acceptance criteria and release readiness.
- Stakeholders: provide context, approvals, and strategic direction.

For detailed responsibilities, see the [OctoAcme Roles and Personas guide](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Common Delivery Cadence

- Daily standups: align on progress, blockers, and dependencies.
- Weekly PM and PdM sync: review priorities, risks, and decisions.
- Delivery check-ins: discuss execution progress, quality, and milestone health.
- Stakeholder updates: share status, risk, and decisions needed.
- Sprint or milestone demo: validate outcomes and collect feedback.
- Retrospective: capture learnings and convert them into improvement actions.

## Key Project Artifacts

- Project charter or one-pager
- Prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria
- Milestone and release plan
- Risk register
- Definition of done
- Retrospective notes and action items
